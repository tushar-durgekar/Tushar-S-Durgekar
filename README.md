Adani Enterprises Stock Market Analysis Dashboard.
Power BI Stock Market Dashboard using Adani Enterprises data
This project presents an end-to-end stock market analysis of Adani Enterprises Limited using a fully interactive Power BI dashboard. The objective of the project was to explore stock price movement, volume behavior, quarterly patterns, and 52-week high/low metrics to understand overall market performance and trading activity across different time periods.

The dashboard provides a clean and detailed representation of multiple financial indicators. Users can filter the data by Day, Month, Quarter, and Year, enabling dynamic insights across various time frames. A set of KPI cards highlights the core performance metrics, including Average 52-Week High, Average 52-Week Low, Total Close Value, Total Open Value, and Total Volume Traded (876M). These KPIs make it easy to understand the market trend at a quick glance.

A breakdown of Quarterly Aggregates of 52-Week High and Low visually shows how the stock behaves across Q1–Q4, allowing comparisons of high-pressure and low-pressure periods in the market cycle. Similarly, the Monthly Aggregates Chart presents 52-week highs/lows with volume movement each month—helping identify months with unusual spikes or dips in trading behavior.

One of the important insights observed from this dashboard is that May, August, and June recorded the highest trading volumes, indicating periods of strong market participation. The dashboard also includes a Daily Trend Line Chart, showing Open vs Close price movement across the entire month. This provides clarity on daily volatility and highlights days with large deviations between market opening and closing values.

Additionally, the analysis includes a Weekday Performance Visualization, showing how the stock behaves on different days of the week—useful for identifying consistent trading patterns.

From business perspective, this dashboard helps investors, analysts, and decision-makers understand price stability, volatility cycles, and trading behavior. It also provides valuable insights for identifying profitable entry/exit points, monitoring investor interest through volume trends, and comparing market strength across different quarters.

Overall, this project demonstrates strong skills in Power BI, data modeling, DAX, data visualization, financial analysis, and interactive dashboard development using real-world stock data.
